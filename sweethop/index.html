Common questions

Why does the game need an internet connection?

Sweet Hop is free and paid for by adverts, and it uses Game Center and the App Store. Without a connection the game shows a notice and continues as soon as you are back online.

Why did my journey start in a different city?

On first launch the game can ask for your location once, so the tour starts in your own country — and in your own city when it is one of the cities in the game. If you decline, a country is picked at random.

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives. A life is lost only when you lose a level; one comes back every 30 minutes, even while the game is closed. A rewarded video gives one more.

I bought something and it has not arrived.

Purchases are handled by Apple. Force-quit and reopen the game first. For No Ads and the Traveler Pass, the shop also has Restore Purchases. If something is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I change my advert consent choice?

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, open the in-level Settings and tap Privacy options. Elsewhere the tracking permission lives in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on the device, so deleting the app deletes it. There is no account, which also means there is nothing of yours on our side.

Can I see the tutorial again?

Yes — tap the ? button on the main menu, or Replay the guide in the in-level Settings.

Privacy

Sweet Hop has no server and collects nothing for us. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
